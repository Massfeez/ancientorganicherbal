Questions we are often asked

Can't find your answer? Message us on WhatsApp and we will help.

Are your products registered with NAFDAC?

Yes. Each product has its own NAFDAC registration number, printed in full on the pack. You can look our products up by name on the NAFDAC Greenbook.

How do I place an order?

Use the Order button on any product to message us on WhatsApp, or call +234 703 069 8595. Tell us the product and the quantity you need and we will confirm availability and delivery.

Do you sell wholesale?

Yes. We supply pharmacies, patent medicine vendors, retail shops and distributors. Send a distributor application and our sales team will get in touch.

Can you produce a custom blend or private label product?

Yes. We develop capsules, teabags and herbal blends to your brief and can pack them under your own branding. Send a request with your requirements and we will respond with a quote.

Where do I find the batch number and expiry date?

Batch No., MFD (manufacture date) and EXP (expiry date) are stamped on the back panel of the pack. Check them before you buy or stock.

Are the products suitable for everyone?

Herbal products affect people differently. Follow the directions on the pack, and ask a doctor or pharmacist before use if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking other medicines or managing a health condition. Our products do not replace medical treatment.
